Cracks

Title:
Cracks
Description:
Painting / lamp.
Size:
20 cm x 20 cm
Medium:
Crackling paste on canvas, LED strip glued to a flame-retardant board and attached to back of the canvas with a velcro.
Date:
March 2025

More crackle paste! But now it’s backlit. Here is what it looks like in daylight.

The initial version consisted of the canvas and an LED strip glued to cardboard. I figured this was not the biggest brain move, so I instead got some fancy fireproof material from the local hardware store and glued the LEDs to that.

I wanted to be able to remove the lights from the canvas, so I used adhesive velcro strips to attach the fancy material to the frame.

I put some black insulation tape to the sides in order to block the light from coming out.
