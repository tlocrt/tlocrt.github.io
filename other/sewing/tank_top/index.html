Tank Top

Title:
Tank Top
Description:
A tank top with pointy sides, made for my mum.
Fabric:
Cotton jersey
Date:
August 2024

My mum was visiting and I made her a tank top. She wanted a tank top with longer sides, similar to one she already has.

The most challenging part of this project was the bottom hem. Since the sides are longer, the bottom edges were not perpendicular to the side seam and I had to improvise sewing those corners. I guess they turned out okay given my (lack of) XP.
