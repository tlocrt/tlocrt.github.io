tlocrt

tlocrt is a solo music project combining indie pop with cinematic and metal-inspired elements. With arrangements built around female vocals, piano, guitars, and electric cello, it aims to bring together atmospheric, orchestral, and heavier sound. Started in late 2025, the project explores personal experiences, as well as broader societal themes.

It can be listened to on all major streaming services.
