A company making virtual instruments organised a competition where they made available a ~70 second long video without audio and asked people to re-score it. The video is a trailer for one of their sample libraries and the original version (with the original audio) is here.

I had a lot of fun working on my version of the score! I’m super happy with how the foley glass/paper sounds at 00:13 turned out and also how the “hopeful” part at 00:25 transitions into creepy.
